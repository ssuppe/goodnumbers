* **Potential Causes Beyond Standard List:**
    * **Hormonal Fluctuations:**  While counter-regulatory hormones contribute to dawn phenomenon, other hormonal variations during the night (e.g., changes in growth hormone or melatonin) could influence insulin sensitivity and contribute to unexpected lows.
    * **Autonomic Neuropathy:**  While less common in well-controlled patients, autonomic neuropathy can affect glucose counter-regulation and contribute to hypoglycemia unawareness, making nighttime lows particularly dangerous.  This should be considered if lows are frequent and asymptomatic.
* **Recommendations:**

    3. **Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM):** If not already using one, strongly recommend a CGM to provide real-time glucose data and alerts for both highs and lows, particularly overnight.

**4. Difficult or Technical Points:**
