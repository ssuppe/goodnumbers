* **Pattern 1 (15:38): 6 High Events:** This suggests a potential issue with the basal insulin rate or insulin sensitivity factor (ISF) in the mid-afternoon.  The timing may also coincide with a regular afternoon snack or activity, warranting further investigation.

* **CGM Data Review with Healthcare Provider:**  Share this detailed analysis with the endocrinologist and diabetes nurse specialist for personalized adjustments to insulin pump settings.

**Specific Recommendations (Addressing Patterns):**

* **Patterns 2 & 5 (Dinner-Related Highs):** Evaluate carb ratios and pre-bolus timing.  Experiment with earlier bolusing and adjusting bolus amounts based on meal composition.  Consider the impact of fat and protein on postprandial glucose excursions.
* **Pattern 3 (Nocturnal Highs):** Adjust basal rates overnight, considering the potential influence of the Dawn Phenomenon.  Consult with the healthcare provider to rule out other causes of nocturnal hyperglycemia.
* **All Patterns:** Investigate potential lifestyle factors like stress, exercise, illness, and sleep patterns, which can influence blood glucose levels.
